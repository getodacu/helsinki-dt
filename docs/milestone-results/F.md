## Recorded operating observations

The authoritative real integrated soak ran **2026-10-09T21:12:48.425Z–21:42:48.466Z** for **1,800.041 seconds**, with cleanup complete at **21:43:18.568Z**. It used the shared HSL/rail/marine service and two SSE readers, observed a peak of 41 local MMSIs, and ended with 41/41 reports having names and metadata. Marine accepted 1,771 reports, saw 21 duplicates and one same-time conflict, and recorded zero marine rejects, motion/inoperative drops, capacity/budget drops or verifier failures. This is a marine-source result, not a zero-reject claim for every source: the shared HSL diagnostics separately recorded 91 HSL rejects. The run received 61,530 national MQTT messages / 11,623,716 bytes plus 73,243 decoded REST bytes, peaking at 55 messages and 10,489 bytes per second. Three position snapshots and 40 successful metadata requests completed.

The controlled interruption began at **21:22:48.476Z**; REST fallback began one millisecond later. MQTT became eligible to reconnect at **21:23:18.476Z** and recovered at **21:23:39.333Z**. At most one marine socket was open at a time. Each of the two shared SSE readers received 1,800 events and 1,765 marine reports, and their latest states matched. Peak SSE frame size was 68,521 bytes. After idle grace, all shared/marine vehicles, caches, queues, in-flight work, connections, subscribers, consumers and timers were zero.

Real movement was observed for SUOKKI (`230108610`): 236 reports, 158 moving samples, with positions from **21:12:45.833Z** through **21:42:46Z** and speed from 2.366 to 3.910 m/s. MYSTAR (`276859000`) contributed 176 reports and 138 moving samples. Stationary observations totalled 388 reports, including 12 dated positions for MMSI `230044550`. The provider-age p95 is the verifier's 1-second histogram bucket at 2,000 ms (approximately 2–3 seconds); maximum age was 264,386 ms. This is distinct from the separately measured browser delivery p95 of 939 ms.

Across 31 memory samples, heap ranged from 24.07 to 78.69 MB and ended at 39.37 MB with garbage collection visible. RSS rose from 134.17 to 227.26 MB, peaked at 228.23 MB, and the last ten samples stayed between 225.53 and 228.23 MB. This 30-minute run is bounded evidence, not a guarantee against indefinite memory growth.

During the five-minute fixture, sampled heap moved between **17.00 and 52.24 MB** with visible garbage collection, while RSS rose from **118.03 to 238.71 MB**. This short synthetic sample does not establish a long-term memory plateau or production capacity guarantee.

## Implementation and limits

The feature adds bounded REST bootstrap/recovery, one shared Digitraffic MQTT connection, per-MMSI metadata lookup, independent marine status, the 10-second report buffer, water markers, ship search/selection and a route-less inspector. It adds no database migration, runtime dependency or deployment. HSL/AIS overlap remains separate; Class A coverage is partial, fishing vessels are filtered upstream, the current bounds exclude Vuosaari, and the report count is not a complete harbour inventory.

Source contracts, limits and operator commands are in [data contracts](../data-contracts.md), [data access](../data-access.md) and [operations](../operations.md). Bounded diagnostics are stored in ignored `.cache/task-f/`; national raw AIS messages are not retained. This report records local implementation and verification only; deployment remains a separate action.
