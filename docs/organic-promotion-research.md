# Organic promotion research: Helsinki Lens

Checked: **2026-10-10**. Objective: creator visibility, a credible portfolio, third-party showcase links and expert feedback. No paid advertising. Only first-party sources support the factual claims below; proposed pitches are recommendations. Public app availability, submission success and acceptance were not tested. Nothing was submitted and nobody was contacted.

## Execution recommendation

Prepare one reusable creator case study first: the problem, a 30–60 second demo, three screenshots, the open datasets and licensing credits, the creator's specific contribution, one technical challenge and the known limitations. Publish this on the creator's portfolio and use an owned LinkedIn post to point to it; this is a proposed owned-channel strategy, not a verified third-party feature opportunity. Submit the HRI and Digitraffic packages next. After that, send a small number of personalised feedback requests to Forum Virium, Aalto and the Helsinki 3D team. Treat Show HN as a separate human-run option subject to its rules.

Measure credible listings, links to the portfolio, profile visits, useful expert responses and invitations to present. Impressions and app visits are secondary for this objective. No outcome is promised.

## Exclusions and caveats

- Fintraffic's 2026 traffic-demo contest is **closed**: the official page gives a submission deadline of **2026-06-08**. It is evidence of interest in demos, not a current submission opportunity. [Contest](https://www.fintraffic.fi/fi/uutiset/onko-sinulla-vuoden-liikennedatademo)
- The Finnish OSM forum has October 2026 discussions, but its rules prohibit spam and no explicit self-promotion permission was verified. Only consider a mapper-relevant technical discussion, with affiliation disclosed, after checking local moderation expectations. [Forum](https://community.openstreetmap.org/c/communities/fi/67), [guidelines](https://community.openstreetmap.org/guidelines). It is lower priority than the six destinations above.
- No paid directories, outreach blasts, automated community posting, invented recipient details or claims of institutional endorsement are included. Public app deployment and form deliverability remain unchecked.
